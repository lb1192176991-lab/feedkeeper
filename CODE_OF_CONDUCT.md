# Code of Conduct

FeedKeeper is a small open source project, and we want it to stay a friendly place to ask questions, report bugs and contribute.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to self-hosting or to open source.
- Give feedback on code and ideas, not on people. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination and trolling are not.
- Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning when you push back.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people talk about the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this code of conduct. To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/visualfusion/feedkeeper/security/advisories/new) or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
